# Fictional engagement brief

**All names, figures, and assertions are SYNTHETIC case inputs.**

Lattice Harbor Supply sells ordinary stationery to fictional business
accounts. A seller prepares a quote, a credit reviewer checks account
exposure, and a billing coordinator checks commercial completeness. The
fictional sponsor wants fewer ambiguous handoffs, not autonomous billing.

The reference intake contains eight quotes and four accounts. It deliberately
mixes a normal order, a credit exception, a discount exception, an inactive
account, incomplete fields, tiny-value rounding, a negative quantity, and a
terms exception. The original inputs are a snapshot, not records copied from
an enterprise.

## Proposed engagement question

Can a small pure calculation boundary explain *why* a quote needs attention
before someone prepares an invoice? A correct calculation could still fail to
reduce rework. No user study or financial outcome is supplied.

## Sponsor decisions still needed

- Confirm which role owns discount, terms, credit, and tax-data decisions.
- Confirm actual rounding, currency, shipping-tax, and account policies.
- Decide whether and how any later pilot may access a real environment.
- Agree on sampling, consent, baseline period, and stop conditions.

## Measurement design, not results

Propose a two-week authorized pilot only after policy and access approval.
Count quote-readiness lead time from first complete submission to human
disposition; count rework as quotes returned at least once divided by all
submitted quotes. Report incomplete submissions separately. Review arithmetic
agreement, incorrect clean classifications, and user comprehension before any
efficiency claim. Sample size, variability, costs, and savings remain unknown.

Success for this seed is a reproducible reference result and a clear owner
pack. No invoice, credit decision, outreach, contract, payment, or deployment
is part of the starter.
