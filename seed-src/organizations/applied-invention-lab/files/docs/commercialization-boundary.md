# Application assessment boundary

**SYNTHETIC opportunity hypotheses. No customer, revenue, patent, or market
validation is present.**

Possible application: an explainable local worksheet for a person grouping
small reusable kits. A printable assignment and visible constraints may be
more appropriate than a service with accounts, integrations, and data
retention. This is a hypothesis to test, not a business recommendation backed
by customer evidence.

Before external positioning:

- Verify harmless mock geometry and label readability.
- Observe whether an authorized participant can follow and correct the
  instructions with synthetic data.
- Learn whether the planning problem occurs often enough to justify a tool.
- Compare with manual grouping and an ordinary spreadsheet; do not assume
  software is the better answer.
- Establish actual data, support, consent, licensing, and procurement needs.

Candidate disconfirming signals: geometric constraints dominate the scalar
model; operators cannot interpret the assignment; manual grouping is simpler;
input data cannot be obtained reliably; or no meaningful repeated problem
exists. A negative outcome is useful lab evidence.

No novel algorithm, patentability, freedom to operate, savings percentage,
customer willingness to pay, or universal reduction in bins is claimed.
Intellectual-property assessment, interviews, publication, purchases, and
any commercial engagement require separate real owner approval. This seed
does not initiate those activities.
