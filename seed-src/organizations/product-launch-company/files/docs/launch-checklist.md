# Conditional launch checklist

**Reference planning document. No launch step below has been completed.**

| Gate | Required evidence | No-go condition |
|---|---|---|
| Product scope | Agreed boundary and limitations | Copy promises calendars, AI, or optimization that does not exist |
| Numerical behavior | Authored test output and fixture comparison | Required time silently disappears or totals disagree |
| Local operation | File-open walkthrough and asset inspection | Server, CDN, account, or external request required |
| Interaction | Observed keyboard/error/import/export protocol | Blocked output mistaken for feasible; stale export enabled |
| Support | Quickstart and minimal intake reviewed | Private agenda collection required or unsupported SLA promised |
| Packaging | Exact four-file demo inventory and source hashes | Missing relative asset or unreviewed dependency |
| Distribution | Owner-approved surface, audience, content, and rollback | Publication or spending not explicitly approved |
| Learning | Consented minimal feedback plan | Usage, conversion, or validation invented |

Proposed delivery options are a static file bundle or an owner-approved static
site. Neither is provisioned by this seed. After approved delivery, verify
assets and the exact published behavior, then record the actual URL or file
hash. If verification fails, withdraw the delivery rather than claiming
success. Do not create fake telemetry to populate a launch report.

Video is optional. `storyboard.md` is only a storyboard. A future rendered
video needs its own approval, real file, duration check, playback review, and
accurate publication record. Omitting video is preferable to fabricating one.
