# Decision brief structure — synthetic corpus only

This is an operating template with review rules, **not** a completed brief or
a claim about a real publication. Do not remove the SYNTHETIC classification.

## 1. Answer the bounded question

State the as-of date, exact pilot requirement, supported recommendation,
confidence limitation, and decision owner. Distinguish “current corpus does
not support” from “a future product cannot achieve.”

## 2. Evidence spine

For each material statement, use an exact citation such as
`sources/release-notes.csv#row=3`. Label it as:

- **Document claim**: what a synthetic source says.
- **Reproduced calculation**: arithmetic over cited included rows.
- **Inference**: reasoning from specified claims/calculations.
- **Unknown**: a missing observation that could alter the decision.

Narrative and structured descriptions of the same run are not independent
corroboration. Keep acknowledgment and recovered-ID metrics distinct.

## 3. Competing explanations

Compare full-offline readiness, supervised-only feasibility, and possible
schedule slip. Include disconfirming evidence and what would reverse the
preferred explanation. Do not assign invented statistical probabilities.

## 4. Uncertainty and action board

Carry forward unresolved restart, release identity, recovery accounting, and
source-independence questions. For each, specify an owner, a concrete next
artifact/test specification, stopping criterion, and decision dependency.

## 5. Owner gate

A local analytical content check is not pilot, procurement, contact, or
deployment approval. The handoff can recommend a hold or a constrained next
investigation; external effects and any new evidence collection require
separate owner authorization. No real product judgment is implied by this
authored exercise.
