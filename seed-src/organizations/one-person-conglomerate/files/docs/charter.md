# Portfolio operating charter

**Classification: SYNTHETIC reference operating design; not an owner approval.**

One founder has 40 hours and a hypothetical USD 600 planning envelope. Protect
8 hours for administration, recovery, and unexpected work, and USD 100 as an
uncommitted reserve. Discretionary constraints are therefore 32 hours and
USD 500. Reserve is not an invitation to spend. Only three business units may
be active and each may run one experiment in this cycle.

## Decision rights and handoffs

1. Executive opens a five-row intake and resolves scope.
2. Research records falsifiable uncertainties, never synthetic validation.
3. Business units produce their own experiment briefs and stop conditions.
4. Finance reconciles the common worksheet. Engineering reports a bounded,
   repeatable calculation; it does not choose strategy.
5. Go-to-market describes five distinct hypotheses without implying all five
   offers are available. Executive records a decision *proposal* for an owner.
6. Any external action gets a separate owner review of audience, content,
   money, data, and reversibility. This seed grants no such authority.

An active unit should have a small artifact, an observation rubric, and a
stopping rule. A parked unit retains its workspace and evidence trail. Parking
is not business failure. Unallocated time is acceptable: maximizing busyness
would be a different, usually worse, objective.

## Exclusions

No customer records, financial advice, handling of payments, personnel
identities, real location trails, production integrations, or revenue
forecasts. The synthetic USD values are discretionary experiment allowances,
not actual spend, valuations, savings, or sales. Internal reusable outputs may
be linked between tasks, but one team does not absorb another team's content.
