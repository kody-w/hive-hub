# Five distinct unit briefs

Every audience and behavior hypothesis below is **SYNTHETIC and unvalidated**.

## Ledgerleaf Tools — offline file utilities

Hypothesis: a person preparing a small CSV import wants understandable
preflight findings before opening another application. The included
`csv_guard.py` checks headers, row shape, duplicate keys, missing fields, and
formula-looking cells without evaluating them. It is not bookkeeping software.
Candidate `lf-schema` narrows its finding format; `lf-export` explores a
machine-readable handoff. Disconfirm if a five-record sample requires more
explanation than manual inspection. Never use real financial files in the
seed exercise.

## Fieldnote Guides — practical reference publishing

Hypothesis: an original printable supply-drawer reset guide can make an
otherwise vague task easier to begin. Starter drawer: 12 blank index cards,
3 binder clips, 2 empty envelopes, and 1 roll of removable paper labels.
Proposed guide: count, group by purpose, label containers, return items, and
undo labels if the grouping fails. `fn-outline` creates a readable page;
`fn-observation` designs later consented observation. Do not offer equipment
repair, electrical, chemical, or safety instructions.

## Quietbench Templates — accessible office templates

Hypothesis: an accessible purchasing-request form reduces missing context.
Starter fields: requested item, reason, alternatives considered, hypothetical
cost, needed-by window, reviewer question. `qb-keyboard` tests field order and
non-color cues; `qb-catalog` explores a three-template naming system. A
keyboard walkthrough is evidence about a prototype, not certification or
evidence that an employer adopted it.

## Routepaper Planners — printable category planners

Hypothesis: a category-first errand sheet is useful without a navigation app.
Starter errands: return a library book, obtain blank labels, and drop an empty
reusable tote at a fictional collection point. Columns: category, time window,
can-wait, and done. No addresses, geolocation, driving times, or real schedules.
`rp-sheet` builds one printable layout; `rp-interview` designs a future
research invitation. Stop if grouping creates more work than a plain list.

## Tinylesson Studio — offline micro-lessons

Hypothesis: a short file-naming lesson supports transfer to a new example.
Starter names: `notes-final-final.txt`, `drawer-list.txt`, and
`2026-09-18-label-plan.txt`. Teach stable purpose and sortable dates, then ask a
learner to name an unrelated sample without copying a model answer.
`tl-sample` builds a six-minute lesson; `tl-transfer` designs the later
assessment. No learning improvement is claimed before observations exist.
